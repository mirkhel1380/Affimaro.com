HOW AFFIMARO WORKS

A shared link.
A clear next step.

Your program follows a practical workflow. Each side sees the information and actions that belong to it.

  1. 01

    Apply, then set the direction

    The owner reviews the brand’s complete profile. After approval, the brand reviews its program rate and attribution window.

  2. 02

    Welcome a partner

    An affiliate completes their profile and applies. The brand reviews the application.

  3. 03

    Connect the result

    A verified store connection records eligible referrals, orders and refund updates.

  4. 04

    Review and pay

    The brand approves commission and pays directly. Both sides can follow the payment record.

GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Know where
you stand.

Visit the help center
Are the four account types separate?

Yes. Brand accounts manage programs, store connections and partner payments. Affiliate accounts manage their own applications, referral links and earnings. Their dashboards, navigation and access permissions are separate.

Does an affiliate need a brand subscription?

No. An affiliate does not need a business workspace or brand subscription to apply to a program. Each brand decides whether to accept an application.

Who pays the affiliate?

The brand pays directly. Bank and Wise transfers happen outside Affimaro and are recorded here. Supported USD Stripe transfers require the brand’s review and confirmation. Affimaro does not hold affiliate funds.

What can I connect?

The current release includes a WooCommerce plugin, Shopify dashboard sync and a server integration for custom websites. A real store must complete setup and verify its own order and refund workflow.

Is pricing available?

Brand and agency subscriptions are Starter $19, Growth $49 and Business $99 USD per month. Creator and affiliate accounts are free. Business accounts complete owner review before Stripe checkout.
