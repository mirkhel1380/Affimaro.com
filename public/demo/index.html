HOW AFFIMARO WORKS

A shared link.
A clear next step.

Four roles follow a connected workflow, with the right responsibilities and records in each portal.

  1. 01

    Choose your account

    Apply as a brand, affiliate, creator or agency. Verify your email, complete your role’s profile and submit it for owner review before portal access.

  2. 02

    Build the partnership

    Approved brands and agencies set up their program. Affiliates and creators discover participating brands, respond to invitations and apply for the brand’s review.

  3. 03

    Share and connect

    Approved partners use their links and assets; creators can plan content. The business verifies its website connection to record eligible orders and refunds.

  4. 04

    Follow the results

    Partners follow clicks and earnings. Businesses review commission and pay eligible requests. Each portal shows only the records its account can access.

GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Know where
you stand.

Visit the help center
Are the four account types separate?

Yes. Brands manage their program and partner payments. Affiliates manage applications, approved links and earnings. Creators also have their own channel profile and content planner. Agencies organize client contacts and manage their approved represented program. Each role has separate navigation and account permissions.

Are affiliate and creator accounts free?

Yes. Affiliates and creators can use their own portal without buying a business subscription. All four roles complete owner review, and each brand separately reviews applications to its program. Brand and agency accounts use a paid package.

Who pays partners?

The business running the program pays directly. Bank and Wise transfers happen outside Affimaro and are recorded here. Supported USD Stripe transfers require the business’s review and confirmation. Affimaro does not hold partner funds.

Do all four roles need to connect a store?

No. Brands and authorized agencies connect the website for their program, using WooCommerce, Shopify dashboard sync or the custom server integration. They verify its order and refund workflow. Affiliates and creators use approved partner links without connecting a store.

What do the packages cost?

Brand subscriptions are Starter $19, Growth $49 and Business $99 USD per month. Agency subscriptions are Starter $39, Growth $79 and Business $149 USD per month. Affiliate and creator accounts are free. Profile approval happens once before paid checkout; purchasing a package does not require a second application review.
